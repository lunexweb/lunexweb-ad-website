## 💰 Ad Budget Allocation

**Month 1 Testing:**
- Google Ads (Search): 50% (R2,700)
- Facebook/Instagram (Warm traffic): 30% (R1,620)
- Retargeting: 20% (R1,080)

**Month 2-3 (After seeing what works):**
- Double budget on best performer
- Cut or optimize underperformers
- Test new ad creative

**Goal:** Cost per lead under R1,000
**Target:** 10 qualified leads per month
**Close Rate:** 20-30% = 2-3 new clients/month
